## Automated Idea Capture System

### The Vision

**From Conversation to Repository:**
> "If you have AI flow coming in, that it picks out all the different ideas that you're having, that you're shouting and it just puts the ideas and it creates already a folder, it creates a Git repository. It starts already collecting all the additional information."

**Workflow:**
1. **Record conversation** (always on)
2. **AI extracts ideas** from transcript
3. **Auto-creates Git repo** for each idea
4. **Populates with context** automatically
5. **Categorizes** by type and priority
6. **Starts research** in background

### Benefits

**Never Forget:**
> "Then when you're browsing, you can just, oh yeah, that was something I was thinking like two weeks ago."

**Automatic Organization:**
- Ideas categorized
- Context captured
- Research started
- Ready to review

**Low Effort:**
- Happens automatically
- No manual note-taking
- No transcription needed
- Just record and forget

## The Idea Research Pipeline

### Stage 1: Idea Capture

**Input Sources:**
- Recorded conversations
- Meeting transcripts
- Chat messages
- Random thoughts

**AI Processing:**
- Identify distinct ideas
- Extract key concepts
- Determine category
- Assess novelty

### Stage 2: Automatic Research

> "Once you have enough information to start a search, to collect all the information about that project, you let it do a deep research automatically."

**Deep Research Includes:**
> "Give me everything about this. So you start your project already with a head start."

**Research Questions:**
1. **Does this already exist?**
   - Existing solutions
   - Competitors
   - Similar projects

2. **What's the market?**
   - Who needs this?
   - Market size
   - Pricing models

3. **What technology?**
   - Best tools for this
   - Open source options
   - Integration points

4. **What's the effort?**
   - Complexity assessment
   - Time estimate
   - Resource requirements

5. **What's the value?**
   - Problem it solves
   - Potential revenue
   - Strategic importance

### Stage 3: Idea Evaluation

**Decision Points:**
> "Is it still a good idea or not? You get into a decision moment."

**Possible Outcomes:**
1. **Pursue:** Move to prototype
2. **Later:** Good idea, wrong time
3. **Archive:** Interesting but not viable
4. **Abandon:** Not worth pursuing

**Informed Decision:**
> "You know already your competition, what to compete against."

## Lessons Learned Database

### Capture Mistakes

> "Failure is always an option. If you learn something from it, that's the most important part."

**Document:**
- What was tried
- What went wrong
- Why it went wrong
- What was learned
- What to do instead

### Example from Discussion

**Celery Workers:**
> "We lost a lot of time on that while there was already an open source tool that did that way better."

**Lesson Captured:**
- Technology: Celery workers for background jobs
- Problem: Expensive and slow
- Alternative: Spark/DataBricks
- Learning: Research open-source alternatives before implementing
- Impact: Week+ of wasted time

**Future Benefit:**
- Won't make same mistake
- Research template created
- Team knows to ask "what exists already?"

### Share Lessons

**Documentation:**
- Personal lessons → Team lessons
