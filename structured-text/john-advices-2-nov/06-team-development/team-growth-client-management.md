### The Scope Creep Problem

**What Happened:**
> "The customer was asking, 'okay, now add this, now add this and now add this.' From the beginning they were like, 'yeah, we need to upload XLS.' Then, 'okay, our files are this big.' Then, 'now we need parquet.' Then they want to upload files that are up this line."

**Frustration:**
> "Couldn't you fucking tell us that from the beginning? If we knew that in the beginning, we could go directly to parquet."

**Why It Happens:**
> "The customer didn't say anything about it. And we didn't research that."

**Two-Part Problem:**
1. Customer doesn't communicate full requirements
2. We didn't ask the right questions upfront

### Learning: Deep Client Research

**Before Starting:**
- What's their real scale?
- What are their constraints?
- What are their future needs?
- What's their technical context?

**Questions to Ask:**
- "What's the largest file you'll process?"
- "What formats do you use?"
- "What's your growth plan?"
- "What systems does this integrate with?"
